# Backlink Kit — Giftora (gift-ora.online)

Publish these to start building links today. Replace `[YOUR_PHONE]`, `[YOUR_ADDRESS]`, `[YOUR_CITY]` with your real details. Never pay for links — these are all free/safe.

## 1. Google Business Profile  (biggest + fastest link)
1. Go to https://business.google.com → create profile → verify (postcard/call).
2. Website field: `https://gift-ora.online/`
3. Business name: `Giftora` (or whatever your storefront name is)
4. Description (copy-paste):
> Giftora delivers birthday cakes, flowers, teddy bears, chocolates & gift combos across [YOUR_CITY] with same-day delivery. Shop by occasion — birthday, anniversary, wedding, housewarming, baby shower, corporate, congratulations, thank-you, engagement & graduation gifts. Order online at gift-ora.online.
5. Categories: `Gift Shop`, `Flower Delivery`, `Cake Shop`.
6. Add 5-10 real product photos + your logo. Reviews: ask 5 happy customers to leave one (review links help rankings a lot).

## 4. Vendor / supplier link (highest value — they list "we supply to")
Email to your gift/cake/flower suppliers (adjust to be honest):

> Subject: Feature your brand on our site
>
> Hi [Vendor],
>
> We run gift-ora.online — an online gift store that regularly orders [cakes/flowers/hampers] from your business for our customer deliveries.
>
> We'd love to feature you on our site as our trusted supplier for [product] with a short "Quality from [Vendor]" note and a link to your page. In return, we'd appreciate if you could add gift-ora.online to your "Clients / We supply to" list on your site.
>
> It's a one-time change on both sides and helps both our SEO. Happy to draft the text for you.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name] — [YOUR_PHONE]
