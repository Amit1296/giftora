## Timing tips that actually convert leads

- **IndiaMART:** buyers contact 4–5 sellers at once; the first real reply usually wins. Reply within minutes, not hours.
- **JustDial:** many enquiries are "just checking price" — always end every message with a question so they keep replying.
- **Never send just "price kya hai?" answers** — price alone loses the sale. Always attach 1–2 alternatives + a reason to order now (same-day cutoff, festival urgency).
- Save every customer's number and re-message before Diwali with a festival offer — repeat customers cost nothing to acquire.
