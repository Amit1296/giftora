Buying guide

Gift combos: more value, more impact, one delivery

A combo bundles two or three gifts into a single delivery, which almost always lands better than one item alone. The classic rose-and-cake pairing is unbeatable for romance, while a teddy plus chocolates suits birthdays and a plant plus dry fruits works beautifully for housewarmings.

Combos also simplify logistics: one order, one delivery slot and one beautifully wrapped parcel. Build yours around the occasion first, then add a personalised note to make it feel genuinely personal.

Building a combo people remember

  • Lead with the occasion, then choose one hero gift and one supporting extra.
  • Roses plus cake is the safest romantic combination.
  • Teddy plus chocolates works for nearly any birthday.
  • A plant plus dry fruits is ideal for housewarming and corporate gifting.
