Buying guide

How to order the right cake for the occasion

Choosing a cake comes down to three things: flavour, size and timing. Chocolate truffle and black forest remain the safest crowd-pleasers, red velvet and butterscotch suit celebrations, and fruit or vanilla cakes work for daytime office events.

For size, allow roughly half a kilogram for every four to five guests. Eggless options are available across most of the range and taste just as rich, and adding a personalised message turns any cake into a centrepiece.

Cake ordering checklist

  • Budget about 0.5 kg per four to five guests to avoid running short.
  • Chocolate and black forest are the safest flavours for mixed groups.
  • Eggless versions are available and taste equally rich.
  • Order a day ahead for custom messages and midnight delivery.
