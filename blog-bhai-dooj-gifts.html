Bhai Dooj gifts for the bond you share

Fifteen Bhai Dooj gift ideas for a brother or sister — sweets, dry fruits, flowers, cakes and combos, delivered the same day in Delhi NCR.

Giftora Team·Sep 22, 2026·6 min read
See gift ideas

Bhai Dooj — the fifth and final day of Diwali, when sisters apply tilak to their brothers, pray for their long lives, and brothers respond with gifts and blessings — falls on Tuesday, 10 November 2026. Like Raksha Bandhan but at the close of the festival week, Bhai Dooj is a day reserved purely for the brother-sister bond. This guide covers fifteen Bhai Dooj gift ideas for both brothers and sisters, from traditional sweets and dry fruits to flowers and cakes — all available with same-day delivery in Delhi NCR and major metros.

Why gift on Bhai Dooj?

Bhai Dooj is the mirror of Raksha Bandhan: sisters mark their brother's forehead with tilak and sweets follow, while brothers respond with gifts and a promise of protection. Gifting on Bhai Dooj is now as popular as Rakhi — brothers send flowers, dry fruits and sweets to their sisters, and sisters send carefully chosen presents to brothers living in another city or abroad. An online Bhai Dooj gift keeps the ritual alive even when the two of you can't be in the same room.

15 best Bhai Dooj gift ideas

  • 1. Traditional sweets box — The Bhai Dooj classic. A box of assorted mithai is the one gift every sibling expects on tilak day.
  • 2. Dry fruit & sweets hamper — Premium dry fruits and festive mithai arranged together — a richer, more gift-worthy box than sweets alone.
  • 3. Fresh flower bunch — A hand-tied bouquet is the most-loved remote sibling gift, especially for a sister living away from home.
  • 4. Flower + cake combo — Roses paired with a half-kg celebration cake — the best-selling Bhai Dooj pairing after the sweets box.
  • 5. Celebration cake — Cut a cake together on the video call if you're apart; a freshly baked truffle cake makes the day festive for the whole family.
  • 6. Premium chocolate box — An easy, universally loved upgrade over mithai, especially when the sibling is younger.
  • 7. Grooming & personal-care hamper — A thoughtful pick for a brother — a grooming essentials hamper he'll actually use every day.
  • 8. Scented candles & home decor — A cosy choice for a sister's home — scented candles and decor accents she'll keep long after the day.
  • 9. Indoor plant — A money plant or peace lily as a symbol of a growing, lasting relationship.
  • 10. Teddy bear with flowers — The playful-budget option that works for either sibling and never misses.
  • 11. Personalised mug or photo frame — A keepsake with a shared memory printed on it — the gift that outlasts every box of mithai.
  • 12. Accessories — watch, sunglasses or belt — For a brother who has everything sweet, a practical accessory he'll carry everywhere works best.
  • 13. Family celebration hamper — Combine sweets, dry fruits and flowers so the whole joint family celebrates together.
  • 14. Gift for the celebrations at home — Add a small gift for the niece or nephew so the little ones feel part of Bhai Dooj too.
  • 15. Last-minute same-day delivery gift — Tilak day caught you off guard? Order before 5 PM and Giftora hand-delivers the same day across Delhi NCR.

Bhai Dooj gifts by budget

  • Under ₹500 — Sweets boxes, flower bunches, chocolates, scented candles and small plants.
  • Under ₹1,000 — Dry fruit-sweets hampers, flower-cake combos, teddies, personalised mugs and photo frames.
  • Premium ₹1,000+ — Grand hampers, accessories, premium chocolates and family celebration cakes.

Gifting tips that make it personal

  • Schedule the delivery for tilak time on 10 November so sweets arrive with the ceremony.
  • For a sister living away, flowers with a handwritten note travel best — she'll read it on the day, wherever she is.
  • Pick the gift to match the sibling — a grooming hamper for a brother, decor and candles for a sister.
  • Sending from abroad? Order online, pay by international card, and we still deliver the same day in India.
