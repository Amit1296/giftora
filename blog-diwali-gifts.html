Diwali gifts they'll love

Twelve thoughtful Diwali gift ideas for family, friends and colleagues — dry fruit hampers, diyas, flowers, cakes and combos, delivered the same day in Delhi NCR.

Giftora Team·Sep 7, 2026·6 min read
See gift ideas

Diwali — the festival of lights — falls on Sunday, 8 November 2026 across India, with the five-day celebration running from Dhanteras to Bhai Dooj. Homes glow with diyas and candles, families gather for Lakshmi Puja, and gifting is at the heart of it all: sweets, dry fruits, decor and thoughtful surprises. If you are wondering what to gift this Diwali, this guide brings you twelve of the best Diwali gift ideas — from classic dry fruit hampers to flower and cake combos — all available with same-day delivery in Delhi NCR and major metros.

Why gift on Diwali?

Diwali is a festival of generosity — handing out sweets, sharing prosperity and strengthening bonds with family, friends, neighbours and colleagues. A thoughtful Diwali gift carries a blessing of good fortune for the year ahead. For families gathering for puja, a dry fruit hamper or a fresh flower arrangement turns the celebration into a memory; for colleagues and clients, a tasteful hamper is a sign of goodwill and respect.

12 best Diwali gift ideas

  • 1. Dry fruit & sweets hamper — The festive classic. Premium assorted dry fruits, kaju katli and a jar of saffron-flavoured sweets in one elegant box make the perfect Diwali mithai gift.
  • 2. Fresh flower bouquet & garland — Marigold garlands and fresh rose bouquets for the puja room double beautifully as a warm gift for family elders.
  • 3. Celebration cake — Turn Diwali dinner into a sweet finale with a freshly baked chocolate truffle, pineapple or black forest cake.
  • 4. Roses + cake combo — A bouquet of red roses with a half-kg celebration cake in one hamper — the easiest way to say "Happy Diwali" and mean it.
  • 5. Diyas, candles & lanterns — Painted diyas, scented candles and paper lanterns brighten every room. Add them as decor alongside a food gift for the perfect double surprise.
  • 6. Chocolates & roses hamper — Ferrero Rocher and chocolate assortments wrapped with fresh roses are the modern, kid-proof Diwali gift.
  • 7. Lucky indoor plant — A money plant or peace lily carries the Diwali spirit of growth and prosperity into a new home or office shelf.
  • 8. Soft teddy bear — Adorable for younger members of the family — every Diwali gift table needs one.
  • 9. Scented candles & diffusers — For friends who love a cosy home, candles in festive scents make a calm and elegant present.
  • 10. Gift for the whole family — Combine sweets, dry fruits, decor and flowers into one grand hamper that the entire household can enjoy.
  • 11. Corporate / client hamper — Send premium dry fruit and chocolate boxes with a printed note to clients and business partners before the holiday rush.
  • 12. Last-minute same-day delivery gift — Forgot a gift? Order before 5 PM and Giftora hand-delivers the same day across Delhi NCR — even the evening of Diwali itself.

Diwali gifts by budget

  • Under ₹500 — Rose hand bunches, chocolate boxes, scented candles and small plants.
  • Under ₹1,000 — Dry fruit boxes, flower + cake combos, and festive hamper sets.
  • Premium ₹1,000+ — Grand dry fruit hampers, luxury combos and large arrangements for family gatherings and corporate gifting.

Gifting tips that make it personal

  • Add a Diwali message that mentions the person's name — generic "Happy Diwali" wishes are easily forgotten.
  • Order a day early so the gift arrives before the Lakshmi Puja on 8 November.
