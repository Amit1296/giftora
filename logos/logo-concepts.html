Giftora — Logo Concepts

Six directions using your brand palette: purple #7c3aed, gold #f5b301, cream #fdf6f8. Concept 6 is the one live on the site.

Concept 1

Gift-Box Monogram

A rounded gift box with a gold ribbon and bow. The white serif "G" anchors the brand name.

Concept 2

Script Wordmark + Bow

Keeps your current lockup — "Gift" in Fraunces, "ora" in Dancing Script — with a gold bow floating above.

Concept 3

Tied-Ribbon Emblem

A circular seal with "GIFTORA" on the ring and a wrapped gift at the core. Feels premium — great for packaging and stamps.

Concept 4

Serif "G" with Gold Heart

One bold Fraunces "G" whose tail curls into a gold heart. Minimal, modern, great favicon.

Concept 5

Wrapped Gift + Smile

A wrapped gift whose ribbon loop forms a handle and a gold smile under it — "delivered with love".

Concept 6 — LIVE on the site

All-Categories Orbit

Your gift box sits at the centre while all 12 category items orbit around it — clothes, toys, flowers, cakes, shoes, teddy, plants, combos, offers, sunglasses, caps, belts.
