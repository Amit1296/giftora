Gift guide

How to plan a birthday gift they will actually remember

A great birthday gift starts with one question: what does this person use every day? Everyday objects - a warm hoodie, a leather belt, a favourite fragrance, a plant for their desk - beat novelty items because they keep giving long after the candles are blown out.

Then layer on the moment. A cake with a personalised message, a bunch of flowers and a handwritten note turn a simple present into an occasion. If you are ordering from another city or country, schedule the delivery for the morning of the birthday so the surprise lands at the start of their day.

Birthday gifting ideas by relationship

  • For a partner: roses or an orchid with a rich chocolate cake.
  • For parents: a plant, a photo gift or a dry-fruit hamper.
  • For a friend: a fun combo of chocolates, a teddy and a handwritten note.
  • For a colleague: keep it simple with chocolates or a desk plant.
  • Order before 4 PM for same-day delivery across Delhi NCR.
