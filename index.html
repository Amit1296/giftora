Why Giftora

Online gift shop India with same-day delivery

Giftora is an online gift store based in Delhi with same-day gift delivery across Delhi NCR and major metros, and express delivery across India. Whether you're looking for a birthday cake delivery in Faridabad, a flower bouquet for your anniversary in Noida, or a teddy bear surprise for your friend in Gurugram, we make it easy to send gifts to your loved ones. Our curated collection includes fresh flowers, designer cakes, soft toys, stylish clothes, plants and gift combos — all beautifully gift-wrapped with a personalised card. We also help NRIs in the USA, UK, Canada and Australia send gifts to India for birthdays, Rakhi, Diwali, Valentine's Day and every celebration in between. With secure online payments, transparent pricing and a dedicated support team, Giftora is the trusted online gift shop for thousands of families across India.

Can I order gifts online with same-day delivery?

Yes. Giftora offers same-day gift delivery across Delhi NCR and major metros, and express delivery across India on all orders placed before 5 PM.

Can I send gifts to India from abroad?

Yes. NRIs in the USA, UK, Canada and Australia can send gifts to India online with a personalised note. We accept international credit and debit cards.

Do you gift wrap the products?

Yes. Every gift comes beautifully wrapped with a personalised card, ready to surprise your loved one.

Which gift is best for a birthday?

Cakes, teddy bears, flowers and gift combos are our most popular birthday gift delivery options. You can also personalise a photo cake or add a handwritten note.

Do you deliver midnight cakes in Delhi NCR?

Yes. We offer midnight cake delivery across Delhi NCR — including Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad and Ghaziabad. Order before 6 PM for midnight delivery.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, wallets and international cards. All payments are 100% secure.

Can I track my order after placing it?

Yes. You'll receive a confirmation email and WhatsApp message with order details. Contact us anytime for real-time updates on your delivery.

Do you offer gift delivery on weekends and holidays?

Yes. Giftora delivers 7 days a week, including weekends and public holidays, so you never miss a celebration.
