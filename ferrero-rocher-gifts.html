What's inside

A Ferrero Rocher box, built around the moment

The pralines come first

Each box is built around sealed Ferrero Rocher packs — whole hazelnut, crisp wafer, smooth chocolate. The number of pralines is stated on the product page, so you know exactly what is being delivered.

Then the pairing

Roses for an anniversary, a cake for a birthday, a teddy for a surprise, dry fruit for a festival or corporate order. The pralines set the tone; the pairing decides how personal the gift feels.

And how it arrives

Hand-checked, gift-wrapped and carded, then dispatched in a protective box. Chocolate is heat-sensitive, so summer deliveries travel with insulation and are scheduled to avoid weekend halts.

FAQ

Ferrero Rocher gift box questions

What is a Ferrero Rocher gift box?

It is a ready-to-give box built around Ferrero Rocher pralines — whole hazelnut wrapped in a crisp wafer and chocolate. We pair the pralines with fresh roses, a cake, a teddy bear or dry fruit to make a complete gift, then wrap it and add a personalised message card.

How much does a Ferrero Rocher gift box cost?

Our Ferrero Rocher gift boxes start at ₹1,599 and go up with the size of the arrangement. The live price, including any offer, is shown on each product page, so the figure you order at is the figure you see.

Can I get same-day delivery in Delhi NCR?

Yes. Order before the daily cut-off and we deliver the same day across Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad and Ghaziabad. Mid-night and fixed-time slots can be arranged on WhatsApp when the area allows it.

Do you deliver Ferrero Rocher gift boxes across India?

Yes. Outside Delhi NCR we use express couriers, so most cities receive the box in one to three days. Chocolate is heat-sensitive, so summer orders travel with insulation and we schedule them to avoid weekend halts.

Can the gift box be personalised?

Every order includes a free message card. You can also build a custom combination — extra roses, a larger cake, a different teddy — by messaging us on WhatsApp before ordering.

Is the Ferrero Rocher in your boxes genuine?

Yes. Our boxes use sealed Ferrero Rocher packs. If you need a specific pack size or a particular assortment, tell us before ordering and we will confirm availability.

Delivery

Where we deliver Ferrero Rocher gifts

Same-day in Delhi NCR, express courier to the rest of India. Chocolate orders are scheduled so they do not sit in a depot over a weekend.
