Dhanteras gifts that bring prosperity

Fifteen auspicious Dhanteras gift ideas — silver coins, diyas, money plants, dry fruit hampers and flowers, delivered the same day in Delhi NCR.

Giftora Team·Sep 22, 2026·7 min read
See gift ideas

Dhanteras — the festival when Hindus worship Goddess Lakshmi and Lord Kubera and buy gold, silver or new utensils as an omen of fortune — falls on Friday, 6 November 2026. Whatever is newly bought or gifted on this day is believed to carry prosperity into the home for the year ahead. This guide lists the fifteen most meaningful Dhanteras gifts, from silver tokens and diyas to dry fruit hampers and new-home decor — all available with same-day delivery in Delhi NCR and major metros.

Why gift on Dhanteras?

Dhanteras marks the beginning of the Diwali week and is considered the most auspicious time of the year to welcome wealth into a home. Gifting metal, a money plant, diyas or festive sweets lets you share that blessing with someone you care about — a family that just moved in, a newly-wed couple, or parents you can't visit this year. For loved ones far away, an online Dhanteras gift is how the tradition travels with you.

15 best Dhanteras gift ideas

  • 1. Silver coin of Lakshmi-Ganesh — The Dhanteras classic. A small silver coin stamped with Lakshmi-Ganesh is the traditional purchase of the day and makes a portable, meaningful gift.
  • 2. Gold-corner accents — A gold-plated idol, pendant or bangle adds the "gold on Dhanteras" tradition on a slightly larger budget.
  • 3. Hand-painted diyas & wick set — The festival begins with lighting lamps; a set of hand-painted terracotta diyas is both a gift and a ritual starter.
  • 4. Scented candles & home fragrance — The modern siblings of the diya, these carry the festival's warm scent through the whole Diwali week.
  • 5. Money plant or lucky bamboo — The prosperity plant. A money plant or lucky bamboo in a decorative pot is a living wish for growth and good fortune.
  • 6. New utensils — Buying new steel, brass or copper vessels on Dhanteras is an age-old custom; a pooja set or serving set makes a beautiful traditional gift.
  • 7. Toran for the front door — A festive marigold or mango-leaf toran welcomes Lakshmi at the doorstep and instantly decorates a new home.
  • 8. Rangoli powder & decor kit — Coloured rangoli powders, flower petals and decorative accents to dress up the entrance before the evening pooja.
  • 9. Fresh flower bunch for the puja thali — Marigold and rose for the thali and the home altar — always needed, always appreciated.
  • 10. Dry fruit & sweets hamper — Premium dry fruits and festive mithai, arranged as a hamper — the edible blessing the whole family shares.
  • 11. Celebration cake for the evening — A freshly baked cake is now a favourite Diwali-eve dessert, especially when the family is celebrating together.
  • 12. Premium chocolate box — An easy, crowd-pleasing add-on to any Dhanteras gift, especially for younger family members.
  • 13. Home decor for the new purchase — Help a sibling, cousin or newly-wed couple decorate the home they've just bought or rented — that is what Dhanteras is really about.
  • 14. Plant + diya combo hamper — A money plant, a set of diyas and scented candles boxed together — a ready-made prosperity hamper.
  • 15. Last-minute same-day delivery gift — Forgot the day? Order before 5 PM and Giftora hand-delivers the same day across Delhi NCR.
