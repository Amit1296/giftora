Online Gifting

Best online gift shop in India

A complete guide to finding the right online gift store in India — what to look for, top gift categories, same-day delivery options, and why Giftora stands out.

Giftora Team·Aug 20, 2026·7 min read
Read the guide

The online gifting market in India has grown rapidly over the past few years. What once required a trip to a local shop can now be done from your phone in minutes — fresh flowers, birthday cakes, teddy bears, plants, fashion accessories and curated gift hampers, all delivered to your doorstep.

But with hundreds of online gift shops competing for your attention, how do you pick the right one? Whether you are sending a birthday surprise across Delhi NCR or gifting a relative in Mumbai, this guide walks you through everything you need to know about choosing the best online gift shop in India.

What makes a good online gift shop?

Not all online gift stores are created equal. Here are the key factors that separate a great gifting experience from a disappointing one:

Wide product range

A good online gift shop should offer something for every occasion and every budget. Look for stores that carry flowers, cakes, plants, soft toys, clothes, accessories, combo hampers and festival-specific gifts — all in one place. The more variety, the easier it is to find the perfect gift without bouncing between multiple websites.

Same-day delivery

Last-minute gift? A reliable online gift shop in India will offer same-day delivery so your gift arrives on time, even if you remembered at the eleventh hour. This is especially important in cities like Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad and Ghaziabad where delivery logistics make it possible.

Gift wrapping included

Nothing kills the excitement of receiving a gift like a plain brown box. The best online gift shops include complimentary gift wrapping so your present looks as special as it feels. A well-wrapped gift shows effort, even when you ordered it from your couch.

Secure payments

Look for stores that accept UPI, credit/debit cards and other secure payment methods. A trustworthy checkout process with SSL encryption protects your financial information and gives you peace of mind.

Customer reviews

Before placing an order, check what other buyers are saying. Genuine customer reviews — on the website, Google or social media — tell you about product quality, delivery speed and how the store handles issues.

NRI-friendly

If you live abroad and want to send gifts to India, the store should accept international credit cards and have a straightforward checkout process for overseas buyers. An NRI-friendly gift shop makes it just as easy to send a gift from the USA as it is from Delhi.

Top gift categories online

Online gift shops in India stock an impressive range of products. Here are the most popular categories:

Cakes and flowers

Fresh flower bouquets and baked cakes remain the top choice for birthdays, anniversaries and celebrations. From classic red rose bunches to exotic orchid arrangements, and from simple vanilla cakes to decadent chocolate truffle — these timeless gifts never go out of style. Many online gift shops deliver fresh cakes and hand-tied bouquets the same day.

Teddy bears and soft toys

Soft toys are a perennial favourite for birthdays, Valentine's Day and "just because" moments. Online stores offer everything from small 6-inch teddies to giant 3-foot plush bears, often paired with chocolates or flowers in a combo gift set.

Clothes and accessories

Fashion-forward gifts like stylish sunglasses, leather belts, trendy caps and sneakers have become hugely popular online. These practical gifts get used every day and make a lasting impression — and they are often more affordable than you would expect.

Plants and green gifts

Plants are the gift that keeps growing. Low-maintenance options like money plants, snake plants, peace lilies and jade plants are perfect for housewarmings, office openings and eco-conscious friends. They purify the air and add a touch of green to any space.

Combo gift hampers

Why settle for one gift when you can send two or three? Combo hampers pair flowers with cakes, chocolates with teddy bears, or dry fruits with rakhis. They feel more generous than their price tag suggests and save you the hassle of placing multiple orders.

Festival-specific gifts

India's festivals drive a huge portion of online gifting. Raksha Bandhan brings demand for rakhis and sweet combos. Diwali sees a surge in dry fruit hampers and gift boxes. Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Father's Day and Christmas all have dedicated collections on top online gift shops. Choosing a store with curated festival collections saves you time and ensures your gift feels relevant.

Same-day gift delivery in India

Same-day delivery has become a game-changer in the Indian gifting market. Whether it is a forgotten birthday or a last-minute anniversary surprise, you can order before a cut-off time (usually 5 PM) and have the gift delivered the same evening.

Major cities with robust same-day delivery networks include:

  • Delhi — The heart of India's gifting market, with the fastest delivery options and widest product selection.
  • Noida — A rapidly growing city with excellent logistics coverage for same-day surprises.
  • Gurugram — From Cyber City apartments to Golf Course Road offices, gifts arrive the same day across Gurugram.
  • Faridabad — Reliable same-day delivery to residential and commercial addresses.
  • Ghaziabad — Full coverage for same-day gifting, including cakes, flowers and combo hampers.

The key to successful same-day delivery is ordering early. Place your order before the cut-off, double-check the delivery address, and add a personalised note to make it extra special.

How to choose the right online gift shop

With so many options available, here are practical tips for picking the best online gift shop in India:

1

Check delivery coverage

Make sure the store delivers to your recipient's pin code. The best shops cover all of Delhi NCR and many tier-1 and tier-2 cities across India.

2

Read reviews first

Look at Google reviews, social media mentions and website testimonials. Consistent positive feedback on delivery speed and product quality is a strong signal.

3

Compare prices honestly

Cheapest is not always best. Compare similar products across two or three stores, factoring in delivery charges, gift wrapping costs and any hidden fees.

4

Look for personalisation

A personalised message card, custom wrapping or the ability to build your own combo adds a personal touch that makes the gift memorable.
