Buying guide

How to choose flowers that arrive fresh and make an impact

Flowers say what words cannot, and the arrangement matters more than the price tag. A tight hand-tied bunch of roses reads romantic, a mixed seasonal bunch feels cheerful and friendly, and a basket arrangement looks substantial enough for a milestone or condolence.

Freshness is everything, so order from a florist that cuts on the day of delivery. Mention the occasion and we will steer you toward colours that fit - reds for love, whites and lilies for sympathy and formal events, mixed blooms for celebrations.

Flower gifting, done well

  • Roses for romance, lilies and orchids for elegance, mixed gerberas for cheer.
  • Basket arrangements look more impressive than a wrapped bunch for milestones.
  • Avoid strongly scented lilies for hospital or condolence deliveries.
  • Pair flowers with a cake or chocolates to make the moment complete.
