🍎 Teacher's Day · 5 September

Teacher's Day gifts they'll remember

Ten thoughtful Teacher's Day gift ideas for your favourite ma'am and sir — flowers, cakes, plants and combos delivered the same day in Delhi NCR.

Giftora Team·Aug 24, 2026·5 min read
See gift ideas

Teacher's Day in India falls on 5 September, honouring the birthday of Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan — a teacher first, President second. It is the one day of the year set aside to say a simple but powerful sentence: thank you for shaping me. If you are wondering what to give your favourite teacher this year, this guide brings you the ten best Teacher's Day gift ideas — from classic flowers to thoughtful combos — all available with same-day delivery in Delhi NCR and major metros.

Why gift a teacher on Teacher's Day?

A good teacher's influence lasts long after the final bell. A small teacher appreciation gift tells them their effort was noticed. For school students it is a warm gesture; for parents it is gratitude from the whole family; for college students and working professionals, it is often the message an old guru treasures the most.

10 best Teacher's Day gift ideas

  • 1. Fresh flower bouquet — Red roses or bright gerberas are the timeless Teacher's Day gift. A hand bunch wrapped in premium paper says "thank you, ma'am" elegantly.
  • 2. Celebration cake — Turn 5 September into a mini celebration in the staff room with a freshly baked truffle, pineapple or black forest cake.
  • 3. Roses + cake combo — The best-selling pairing: a bouquet of red roses with a half-kg celebration cake in one beautiful hamper.
  • 4. Lucky indoor plant — A money plant or peace lily keeps growing on their desk long after the day — perfect for the teacher who loves green corners.
  • 5. Chocolates & roses hamper — Ferrero Rocher with fresh roses is a sweet upgrade over a box of mithai.
  • 6. Soft teddy bear — Adorable for primary-school teachers who receive hugs all day anyway.
  • 7. Personalised note card — Whatever you order, add a handwritten-style message. Teachers keep these cards for years.
  • 8. Dry fruits gift box — A healthy premium hamper for senior teachers, principals and mentors.
  • 9. Class gift pool — Collect small amounts from the whole class and send one grand combo — bigger surprise, same budget per student.
  • 10. Last-minute same-day delivery gift — Forgot the date? Order before 5 PM and Giftora hand-delivers the same day across Delhi NCR.

Teacher's Day gifts by budget

  • Under ₹500 — Rose hand bunches, single-serve treat boxes and small plants.
  • Under ₹1,000 — Flower + cake combos, chocolate hampers and designer arrangements.
  • Premium ₹1,000+ — Two-tier cakes, grand bloom baskets and luxury hampers for group gifting.

Gifting tips that make it personal

  • Mention a lesson, quote or moment you remember — specific gratitude beats generic wishes.
  • Order for the morning of 5 September so the gift arrives during school hours.
  • Use code TEACHER15 at checkout for 15% off Teacher's Day gifts.
